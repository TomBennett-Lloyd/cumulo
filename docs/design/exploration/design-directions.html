Cumulo — design direction candidates

Three candidate directions for issue #15. Every categorical palette passed the dataviz validator (lightness band, chroma floor, CVD ΔE, normal-vision floor, surface contrast) in both modes; the first three slots also pass the harder all-pairs check used for map markers. Type scale (12/13/15/18/22/28, minor-third-ish) and the 4px-base spacing scale are held constant across candidates — the reaction wanted here is colour, mood, and the band treatment. Each candidate shows: tokens, the forecast uncertainty-band chart treatment (P10–P90 wash at 12% + inner P25–P75 at 16%, dashed 2px forecast median, solid 2px actuals with an end marker, "now" rule), the site-map treatment (default / hover / selected / cluster markers), and the Open-Meteo attribution component in place.
